A clear WOF process

A careful inspection, explained properly.

J.R Automotive is WOF certified. We inspect your vehicle carefully and give you a clear result without making the process more stressful than it needs to be.

If something needs attention, we will explain what was found and what the next step is. No confusing workshop talk and no repair work carried out without discussing it with you first.

Key safety areas

A WOF inspection covers important safety areas such as:

  • Tyres, wheels and brakes
  • Lights and vehicle visibility
  • Steering and suspension
  • Windscreen, wipers and seat belts
  • Body condition and other safety items

What we can inspect

Cars

WOF inspections for everyday passenger cars and light vehicles.

Trailers

WOF inspections for road-going trailers that require a current warrant.

LAMS motorcycles

WOF inspections for LAMS motorcycles. We inspect motorcycles but do not carry out motorcycle repairs.

Common questions

Before your WOF

Calling ahead is the best way to arrange a suitable inspection time.

What if my vehicle does not pass?

We will explain what was found and what needs attention. If it is repair work we offer, we can discuss the options and provide a quote before anything is done.

Can you inspect my trailer?

Yes. We carry out WOF inspections for cars, trailers and LAMS motorcycles.

Do you repair motorcycles?

No. We provide WOF inspections for LAMS motorcycles, but we do not carry out motorcycle repairs.
